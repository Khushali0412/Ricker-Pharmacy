Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions and answers about our IM Shot services.

IM (Intramuscular) Shots are injections that deliver vitamins, minerals, and nutrients directly into the muscle tissue. This allows the nutrients to be absorbed directly into your bloodstream, bypassing the digestive system for near-100% bioavailability—much more effective than oral supplements.

Most IM Shot appointments take just 5-10 minutes. There is no downtime required, so you can return to your normal activities immediately after your injection.

The right IM Shot depends on your specific wellness goals. B12 is great for energy, Vitamin D for immunity and bone health, Glutathione for detox and anti-aging, Lipo-C/MIC for weight management, CoQ10 for heart health, and Vitamin C for immune support. Our pharmacist can help you choose.

IM Shots are generally very safe when administered by trained healthcare professionals. Common minor side effects may include temporary soreness at the injection site, mild redness, or slight bruising. Serious side effects are rare. We'll review your health history before any injection.

Frequency depends on the type of shot and your individual needs. Many patients benefit from weekly or bi-weekly B12 or Lipo-C shots, while others may get monthly Glutathione or Vitamin D injections. Our pharmacist will recommend a schedule based on your goals.

Requirements may vary by state and injection type. Contact Ricker Pharmacy to learn about availability and whether a prescription is needed for your desired IM Shot. Our team is happy to coordinate with your healthcare provider as needed.
