Pediatric Compounding: Kid-Friendly Flavors, Exact Dosing & Stress-Free Medicine

Shawn Nelson, PharmD Compounding Specialist
August 28, 2026 4 min read
Custom pediatric liquid suspensions with kid-approved natural flavors remove the struggle from medication time.

Key Takeaways at a Glance

  • Weight-Based Dosing: Children grow rapidly; compounding provides exact milligram-per-kilogram liquid strengths rather than splitting adult pills.
  • Flavor Customization: Over 20 delicious kid-favorite flavors (bubblegum, strawberry, grape, watermelon) to mask bitter active ingredients.
  • Allergen-Free Formulations: Made 100% free of artificial red dyes, sugar, gluten, alcohol, or dairy.

The Challenge of Giving Medicine to Children

Every parent knows the struggle of trying to get a sick child to swallow a bitter, chalky pill or drink an unpleasant commercial medicine. Many essential pediatric medications are only manufactured in adult pill strengths, forcing parents to crush tablets and guess at fractional dosages.

Pediatric compounding at Ricker Pharmacy turns medication time from a battle into a smooth, tear-free routine.

How Compounding Helps Parents and Pediatricians

Custom Sweet Flavors

Bubblegum, wild cherry, sweet grape, sour apple, and raspberry flavors that completely mask harsh medicinal bitterness.

Precise Liquid Dosing

Converting solid tablets into calibrated oral liquid suspensions with easy-to-measure oral dosing syringes.

Dye-Free & Sugar-Free

Safe formulations for children with ADHD, autism spectrum sensitivities, diabetes, or severe food allergies.

Alternative Child Forms

Formulating medications as medicated freezer pops, gummy chews, dissolvable lozenges, or topical transdermal gels.

"When a child happily takes their medicine on time, they heal faster and parents can finally rest easy. We love creating formulas that make pediatric wellness simple."

— Shawn Nelson, PharmD, Compounding Specialist at Ricker Pharmacy
