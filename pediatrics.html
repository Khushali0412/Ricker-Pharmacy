Turning "Yuck" Into "Yum" For Your Child

Your child and their medication don't always make for a good combination, but with our pediatric compounding at Ricker Pharmacy we can turn "yuck" into "yum." Flavoring bitter antibiotics is just one way we can help.

Since each child's size, weight, allergies, and drug tolerance are different, Ricker Pharmacy can work with you and your child's pediatrician to make sure that the medication prescribed is taken as directed with no muss and no fuss. We can also turn medications into small suppositories for children who can't swallow pills.

Kid-Friendly Medication Forms

We offer a wide variety of creative dosage forms designed specifically for children, making it easy and even fun for your child to take their medication exactly as prescribed.

Flavored Liquids

Bitter medications transformed into great-tasting flavored liquid suspensions that children willingly take without a fuss.

Medicated Lollipops

Flavored medicated lollipops that make medication time feel like treat time—a favorite among our youngest patients.

Flavored Ice Popsicles

Frozen medicated popsicles in kid-favorite flavors that soothe sore throats while delivering precise medication doses.

Topical & Transdermal Creams

Applied directly to the skin for children who have difficulty with oral medications—painless and stress-free delivery.

Troches & Dissolving Tablets

Flavored sublingual troches and rapidly dissolving tablets that melt in the mouth without needing to swallow pills.

Pacifier Bottles & More

Pacifier bottles with medication syringe inserts for infants, plus suppositories, capsules, and effervescent powder packets.

Why Choose Ricker for Pediatric Compounding?

Our compounding pharmacists specialize in creating child-friendly medications that ensure every dose is taken safely, effectively, and without the daily struggle.

Child-Friendly Medication Solutions

Flavored • Fun Forms • Age-Appropriate
  • Taste Masking: We transform bitter-tasting antibiotics and other medications into delicious flavored liquids, lollipops, and popsicles that children actually enjoy taking.
  • Age-Appropriate Forms: From pacifier bottles with medication syringe inserts for infants to rapidly dissolving tablets for older children, we match the form to your child's age and ability.
  • Allergy-Safe Options: We formulate medications free from common allergens, dyes, sugars, and preservatives for children with sensitivities and special dietary needs.

Precision & Personalized Care

Weight-Based Dosing • Pediatrician-Coordinated
  • Custom Dosing: Each medication is precisely dosed based on your child's individual size, weight, and drug tolerance—not a one-size-fits-all approach.
  • Physician Collaboration: We work directly with your child's pediatrician to ensure the compounded medication meets the exact therapeutic requirements prescribed.
  • Alternative Delivery: When children can't swallow pills, we offer suppositories, transdermal creams, effervescent powders, and other creative alternatives to ensure effective treatment.

Common questions and answers about our pediatric compounding services.
