Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions and answers about our pediatric compounding services.

We can compound virtually any medication your child's pediatrician prescribes into a child-friendly form. This includes antibiotics, pain medications, allergy medications, and more—transformed into flavored liquids, lollipops, popsicles, troches, creams, suppositories, and other creative forms.

We offer over 14 kid-favorite flavors including Apple, Banana, Bubble Gum, Chocolate, Grape, Grape Bubble Gum, Lemon, Orange, Raspberry, Sour Apple, Strawberry, Watermelon, Marshmallow, and Peanut Butter. Your child can even help pick their favorite!

We offer many alternatives including flavored liquids, medicated lollipops, ice popsicles, sublingual troches that dissolve in the mouth, rapidly dissolving tablets, topical and transdermal creams, suppositories, effervescent powder packets, and pacifier bottles with medication syringe inserts for infants.

Absolutely! We specialize in creating medications that are free from common allergens, artificial dyes, sugar, preservatives, lactose, and gluten. We tailor each formulation to your child's specific allergies and sensitivities.

Since each child's size, weight, allergies, and drug tolerance are different, we work with your child's pediatrician to calculate the exact dosage needed. The medication is then compounded to deliver that precise dose in whichever form works best for your child.

Simply have your child's pediatrician send a prescription to Ricker Pharmacy. Our compounding team will then work with you to choose the best dosage form and flavor for your child. You can also call us directly to discuss options before your next doctor's visit.
