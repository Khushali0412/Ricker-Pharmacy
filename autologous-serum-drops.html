Specialty Medicine With Commitment, Compassion, And Care.

Introducing ASEDs: dry eye drops derived from your own blood. If you are one of those patients who have failed to find long-lasting relief, have you considered autologous serum eye drops (ASED) or AST Drops (Autologous Serum Drops).

The use of autologous serum eye drops dates back to the 1980’s when they were first used to treat chemical burns of the eye. ASEDs are made from serum -- a component of your blood that is combined with sterile saline to create a lubricating fluid rich in certain nutrients and growth factors that may be missing in very dry eyes. The term autologous means that it comes from your own body.

What are autologous serum eye drops?

ASEDs are highly effective, well-tolerated drops containing natural elements that artificial tears cannot replicate—such as antibodies, albumin, Vitamin A, and epidermal growth factors—to promote healthy corneal growth and healing.

Antibodies

Essential immune defenders naturally present in your blood serum to protect delicate ocular tissues.

Albumin

A natural protein that creates a superior, protective tear film barrier over corneal surface cells.

Vitamin A

Vital nutrient supporting corneal epithelial integrity, moisture retention, and clear vision.

Epidermal Growth Factors

Special cellular growth factors that stimulate active regeneration and rapid healing of the cornea.

Natural Salinity & pH

Matches patient's own natural tears with identical salinity and pH to ensure zero burning or stinging upon instillation.

100% Biocompatibility

Derived entirely from your own blood with no risk of donor graft-versus-host rejection or intolerance.

Do autologous serum eye drops work?

In our patients with moderate to severe dry eye disease, we have found that ASEDs often help their eyes feel better, see better and, in persons with damaged or operated corneas, heal better.

While theories have been floated as to why ASEDs help dry eye patients who have failed to improve with other therapies, research suggests it is due to serum containing abundant albumins (natural proteins) and critical growth factors that create a superior, protective tear film for corneal surface cells.

How are eye drops made from my own blood cells?

A straight forward and convenient process: a small blood draw prepared under strict aseptic technique produces a 100% personalized, preservative-free tear substitute matching natural tear salinity and pH.

15-Min Blood Draw

01

A small blood sample is collected on-site by a licensed phlebotomist under sterile technique. Fasting is not required.

Centrifuge & Filtration

02

Blood is centrifuged to separate nutrient-rich serum, followed by specialized filtration for bacterial sterilization and clarification.

ISO 5 Sterile Compounding

03

Formulated in an ISO 5 Biological Safety Cabinet (BSC) with preservative-free solution matching natural tear salinity and pH (no stinging).

Cold Storage & Daily Use

04

Stored frozen for up to 6 months. Thaw one vial at a time in the refrigerator for daily application (4x/day) to maintain optimal freshness.

Common questions and answers regarding Autologous Serum Eye Drops (ASEDs).

Autologous serum eye drops (ASEDs) are custom-compounded drops made directly from your own blood serum. Unlike commercial artificial tears, they contain vital natural biological components—including growth factors, vitamins, antibodies, and albumin—that replicate the biochemical profile of real human tears to actively heal and lubricate damaged ocular surfaces.

We provide a seamless on-site service at Ricker Pharmacy. A licensed phlebotomist performs a gentle, 15-minute blood draw (no fasting required). We then centrifuge the blood to isolate the nutrient-rich serum and compound your drops under strict aseptic conditions in our certified ISO 5 Biological Safety Cabinet.

Your supply of unopened serum drops is stored frozen and remains fresh for up to 6 months. When ready to use, simply thaw one vial at a time in the refrigerator for daily application (typically 4 times a day). An active refrigerated vial maintains optimal freshness for up to 7–10 days.
