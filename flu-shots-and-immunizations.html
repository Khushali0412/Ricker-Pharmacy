Our Comprehensive Vaccine Services

We stock CDC-recommended vaccines for adults, seniors, teens, and travelers to ensure complete preventative care for every stage of life.

Seasonal Influenza (Flu)

Annual quadrivalent flu vaccines for adults and enhanced high-dose flu shots formulated specifically for seniors ages 65 and older to maximize protection.

Shingles (Shingrix)

The CDC-recommended two-dose Shingrix vaccine for adults 50 and older, offering over 90% protection against painful shingles outbreaks and nerve complications.

Pneumococcal (Pneumonia)

Essential pneumonia vaccines (Prevnar 20 / Pneumovax 23) safeguarding seniors and individuals with asthma, diabetes, or chronic conditions against bacterial lung infections.

Tdap / Tetanus Booster

Protection against Tetanus, Diphtheria, and Pertussis (Whooping Cough), highly recommended every 10 years and for expectant mothers and infant caregivers.

COVID-19 Updated Vaccines

Updated COVID-19 vaccines and boosters targeting dominant circulating variants, keeping your immunity robust and reducing the risk of severe illness.

Travel & Specialty Vaccines

Hepatitis A & B, Meningitis, MMR (Measles, Mumps, Rubella), and customized travel consultations to prepare you safely for domestic and international trips.

Specialized Protection for Every Population

Vaccine recommendations vary based on age, lifestyle, and health conditions. We customize your immunization plan to your unique life stage.

Seniors & High-Risk Individuals

Ages 65+ • Chronic Illness • Compromised Immunity
  • High-Dose Senior Flu Vaccine: Formulated with 4x the antigen of standard flu shots to create a stronger immune response in adults aged 65 and over.
  • Comprehensive Pneumonia Care: Essential single and sequential dose pneumonia protocols for individuals with diabetes, asthma, COPD, or heart disease.
  • Shingles Prevention: Two-dose Shingrix regimen providing vital defense against post-herpetic neuralgia and long-term nerve pain.

Workplaces, Schools & Families

Corporate Clinics • Caregivers • Students
  • Family Cocooning: Tdap boosters for expectant parents, grandparents, and caregivers to safeguard newborns against dangerous whooping cough.
  • On-Site Corporate Flu Clinics: We offer convenient employer and workplace vaccination clinics across Jacksonville to minimize sick days and keep teams healthy.
  • College & Vocational Requirements: Fast administration and certified documentation for university, healthcare student, and vocational vaccine mandates.
