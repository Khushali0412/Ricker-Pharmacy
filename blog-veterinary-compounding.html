Veterinary Compounding: Custom Dosages, Flavored Treats & Stress-Free Pet Care

Mitesh Patel, RPh Senior Pharmacist
August 16, 2026 5 min read
Pet-specific flavors and transdermal ear gels eliminate the struggle of giving pills to dogs and cats.

Key Takeaways at a Glance

  • Transdermal Ear Gels: For cats that refuse oral pills, medications can be absorbed directly through the skin of the inner ear flap.
  • Pet-Approved Flavors: Beef, chicken, liver, bacon, tuna, and cheese flavorings turn medicine into an irresistible treat.
  • Species-Specific Precision: Accurate weight adjustments prevent accidental overdose in small kittens, puppies, birds, and exotics.

The Challenge of Medicating Pets

Giving medication to a dog or cat can be a traumatizing experience for both the pet and pet owner. Cats often spit out hidden pills, claw aggressively, or foam at the mouth from bitter flavors. Dogs frequently eat the treat and spit the pill cleanly onto the floor.

Furthermore, many human medications prescribed off-label for pets contain toxic excipients (such as xylitol or propylene glycol) that can cause acute liver failure or hypoglycemia in animals.

Veterinary Compounding Solutions

Chewable Soft Treats

Soft, bite-sized medicated treats flavored with roasted beef, bacon, chicken liver, or cheddar cheese.

Transdermal Ear Gels

Apply a tiny pre-measured click of PLO gel to the hairless inside of a cat's ear flap (pinna) for zero-fuss absorption.

Flavored Oral Liquids

Tuna, salmon, and sardine oil suspensions that cats eagerly lick directly from a spoon or dropper.

Tiny Micro-Capsules

Custom #4 and #5 micro-capsules sized specifically for small 5-pound animals.
