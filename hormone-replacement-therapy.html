Bioidentical Hormone Replacement Therapy (BHRT)

Bioidentical Hormone Replacement is a natural way to treat menopause, premenopause, or PMS symptoms. Bioidentical hormones are exact copies of the hormones your body naturally produces.

Symptoms of Hormone Imbalance

Every woman is unique, and hormone therapy should be too. The one-size-fits-all approach of manufactured synthetic hormones does not work for many women. Recognize these common symptoms and discover how personalized BHRT can help.

Hot Flashes

Sudden intense waves of heat, mostly on the upper body, lasting up to five minutes at a time.

Night Sweats

Disruptive sweating episodes during sleep that interfere with rest and daily energy levels.

Foggy Thinking

Difficulty concentrating, memory lapses, and reduced mental clarity caused by hormonal shifts.

Weight Gain

Unexplained weight gain, especially around the midsection, driven by metabolic hormone changes.

Fatigue & Mood Swings

Persistent tiredness, irritability, anxiety, and unpredictable emotional shifts throughout the day.

Low Sex Drive

Reduced libido and diminished interest in intimacy as a direct result of hormonal decline.

Here's The Breakdown of Your Hot Flashes

Portions of the brain which control body temperature react to dips in the blood level of hormones. Understanding this process helps explain why BHRT is so effective.

Brain Temperature Response

01

The brain's insular cortex, which controls perceptions of heat, cold, pain, and pleasure, activates in response to hormone level dips.

Core Temperature Rises

02

About 20 minutes before a full-blown hot flash, your core body temperature begins to rise. Vessels dilate and blood flow increases to prepare the body.

Heat & Flushing Onset

03

You feel hot, sometimes intensely, mostly on the upper third of your body. Blood rushes to skin across your chest, neck, and face, causing visible flushing.

Thermal Chaos Completes

04

Peripheral blood vessels fully dilate and heat shoots out. Total time of thermal chaos: under five minutes. Flashes are most common in the morning and evening.

Key Hormones in BHRT

Understanding the roles of these essential hormones helps explain why personalized bioidentical replacement therapy is so effective for restoring balance and vitality.

Estrogen (E1, E2, E3)

Primary Female Sex Hormones • Deficiency Causes Most Symptoms
  • Estrone (E1): Accounts for 10-20% of circulating estrogens. Found in equilibrium with E2. Metabolites of E1 are carcinogenic, thus there is virtually no need to ever supplement with E1.
  • Estradiol (E2): Accounts for 10-29% of circulating estrogens and is the strongest form. Found in commercial products such as Estrace®, Vivelle®, and Climara®.
  • Estriol (E3): A weak estrogen accounting for 60-80% of circulating estrogens. Studies indicate Estriol may play a role in breast cancer prevention due to its nonproliferative effects on breast tissue.

Progesterone, Testosterone & DHEA

Essential Supporting Hormones • Balance & Vitality
  • Progesterone: Enhances positive effects of estrogen, counteracts estrogen dominance, regulates menstrual cycles, thyroid and blood sugar levels, and has a natural sedative effect to help counter sleeplessness. Note: Progesterone (bioidentical) is different from Progestin (synthetic).
  • Testosterone: Plays a major role in activating sexual circuits of the brain, promoting a healthy libido. Proper levels help maintain energy, promote a healthy sex drive, and build and maintain bone mass and muscle tone.
  • DHEA & Cortisol: DHEA is a precursor to testosterone that increases feelings of well-being and helps prevent bone loss. Cortisol imbalance from adrenal stress can cause fatigue, weight gain, decreased muscle mass, and sleep disturbances.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions and answers regarding Bioidentical Hormone Replacement Therapy (BHRT).

BHRT uses hormones that are exact copies of the hormones your body naturally produces—such as estrogen, progesterone, testosterone, and DHEA—derived from a natural soy or yam base and custom-compounded to match your individual needs, unlike one-size-fits-all synthetic alternatives.

In 2002, the Women's Health Initiative study was discontinued as risks of conventional synthetic HRT outweighed the benefits. BHRT uses bioidentical hormones that are chemically identical to what your body produces, unlike synthetic progestins and equine estrogens used in conventional therapy. BHRT is individually compounded to your specific hormone levels.

While the phrases are often used interchangeably, NHRT typically refers to weaker plant-based estrogens known as phytoestrogens. While naturally occurring, they are not identical to estrogens produced by the human body. BHRT hormones are structurally identical to human hormones.

BHRT can help alleviate hot flashes, night sweats, mood swings, anxiety, memory lapses, depression, bone loss, weight gain, headaches, decreased sex drive, fatigue, irritability, foggy thinking, and irregular periods associated with menopause, premenopause, and PMS.

Many clinicians feel that patients who have undergone a hysterectomy do not require progesterone supplementation. However, even women who have gone through surgical menopause can benefit, as there are progesterone receptors present all over the body that play important roles beyond reproductive function.
