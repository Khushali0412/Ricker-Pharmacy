What Is Peptide Therapy?

Peptide therapy uses naturally occurring chains of amino acids to support specific functions throughout the body. Depending on the peptide prescribed and your individual health goals, peptide therapy may help support metabolism, recovery, healthy aging, cognitive function, and overall wellness.

Our providers create personalized treatment plans based on your needs and medical history.

What Can Peptide Therapy Help Support?

Healthy Weight Management

Peptide therapy may help support healthy weight loss by promoting fat metabolism while helping preserve lean muscle mass.

Potential benefits include:

  • Support for fat metabolism
  • Preservation of lean muscle during weight loss
  • Improved body composition
  • Reduction of visceral (abdominal) fat

Muscle Recovery & Performance

Certain peptides may help support recovery following exercise or injury, allowing you to stay active and perform at your best.

Potential benefits include:

  • Faster post-workout recovery
  • Improved muscle repair
  • Support for strength training
  • Reduced recovery time

Healthy Aging

Some peptides are used to support healthy aging by promoting skin health and collagen production.

Potential benefits include:

  • Healthier-looking skin
  • Increased collagen production
  • Improved skin elasticity
  • Support for a more youthful appearance

Energy & Vitality

Peptide therapy may help improve overall energy levels and physical performance.

Potential benefits include:

  • Increased energy
  • Improved exercise tolerance
  • Reduced fatigue
  • Enhanced overall wellness

Hormonal Support

Certain peptides work by supporting the body's natural growth hormone production.

Potential benefits include:

  • Healthy growth hormone secretion
  • Improved recovery
  • Metabolic support
  • Better sleep quality

Brain Health

Some peptides are currently being researched for their potential effects on cognitive function.

Areas of ongoing research include:

  • Focus and concentration
  • Mental clarity
  • Memory
  • Cognitive performance

GLP-1 Medications

Semaglutide Tirzepatide Retatrutide

GLP-1 medications are prescription treatments that support weight management and healthy blood sugar regulation by mimicking naturally occurring hormones involved in appetite and glucose control.

Weight Management

  • Clinically significant weight loss
  • Improved body composition
  • Reduced waist circumference

Blood Sugar Support

  • Increased insulin release when blood sugar is elevated
  • Reduced glucagon secretion
  • Improved insulin sensitivity

Cardiometabolic Health

Weight loss achieved with GLP-1 therapy may also contribute to improvements in:

  • Blood pressure
  • Cholesterol levels
  • Overall metabolic health
  • Cardiovascular risk factors

Popular Peptides We Offer

NAD+

01

NAD+ is a naturally occurring coenzyme that plays an essential role in cellular energy production by helping mitochondria generate ATP, the body's primary energy source.

Potential benefits include:

  • Increased energy
  • Reduced fatigue
  • Improved stamina
  • Enhanced physical performance

Tesamorelin

02

Tesamorelin is an FDA-approved peptide used to reduce excess abdominal fat in adults with HIV-associated lipodystrophy. It is also prescribed in other settings based on provider evaluation.

Can assist with:

  • Reduction in visceral fat
  • Improved body composition
  • Preservation of lean muscle
  • Metabolic support

Sermorelin

03

Sermorelin helps stimulate the body's natural production of growth hormone.

Potential benefits include:

  • Improved sleep quality
  • Enhanced recovery
  • Increased lean muscle
  • Reduced body fat
  • Higher energy levels
  • Healthy aging support

Ipamorelin

04

Ipamorelin is a selective growth hormone secretagogue designed to encourage natural growth hormone release.

Potential benefits include:

  • Recovery support
  • Muscle maintenance
  • Improved sleep
  • Support for healthy fat metabolism

CJC-1295

05

CJC-1295 is a long-acting growth hormone-releasing hormone (GHRH) analog that may stimulate the body's natural production of growth hormone.

Potential benefits include:

  • Recovery support

BPC-157 & TB-500

SEMAX

SEMAX is a peptide being studied for its effects on brain-derived neurotrophic factor (BDNF), a protein involved in learning and memory.

Potential benefits include:

  • Mental resilience

GHK-Cu (Copper Peptide)

GHK-Cu is widely used in regenerative and aesthetic medicine for skin and hair support.

Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about peptide therapy and how custom formulations support your health goals.

Peptide therapy utilizes specific sequences of amino acids that mimic the body's natural signaling peptides. These molecules bind to cell receptors and trigger specific biological actions—such as stimulating human growth hormone, repairing damaged tendons, metabolizing visceral fat, or boosting collagen production.

Yes. Because clinical peptides are potent biological compounds, they require an authorized prescription from a licensed healthcare provider (physician, physician assistant, or nurse practitioner). Ricker Pharmacy collaborates closely with prescribers throughout Jacksonville to formulate personalized protocols.

Most peptide therapies are administered via simple, virtually painless subcutaneous micro-injections using tiny insulin-sized needles. However, depending on the specific peptide and treatment goal, some formulations are available as oral capsules, sublingual troches, nasal sprays, or topical creams.

Timelines vary based on the peptide. Patients using BPC-157 for tissue injury or GLP-1s for appetite suppression often notice improvements within 1 to 3 weeks. Growth hormone secretagogues (CJC-1295/Ipamorelin) and collagen peptides typically show progressive improvements in sleep, muscle tone, and skin elasticity over 2 to 6 months.

Peptides are generally well-tolerated because they are natural signaling molecules that stimulate your body's own production rather than introducing high doses of foreign synthetic hormones. This preserves your body's natural feedback loops and significantly minimizes potential side effects.

We source only high-purity Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients from FDA-registered chemical suppliers. All injectable peptides are compounded in our certified cleanroom under strict USP sterile compounding standards, with rigorous batch testing for potency, endotoxins, and sterility.
