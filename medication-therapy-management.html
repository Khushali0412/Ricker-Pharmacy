Personalized Consultations for Safer, Smarter Care

Medication Therapy Management (MTM) is a dedicated clinical service where our pharmacists conduct a comprehensive review of all your prescriptions, over-the-counter medicines, and dietary supplements.

Taking multiple medications prescribed by different doctors increases the risk of drug interactions, duplicate therapies, and unexpected side effects. At Ricker Pharmacy, our clinical pharmacists partner with you and your physicians to optimize your drug regimen, reduce out-of-pocket costs, and ensure every treatment supports your overall health goals.

Comprehensive Medication Reviews (CMR)
Drug interaction & duplicate therapy screening
Cost-saving generic & alternative recommendations
Side effect minimization & dosage optimization
Actionable Personal Medication Record (PMR)
Direct collaboration with your primary care doctor

What Happens During an MTM Consultation?

Our structured clinical review addresses every aspect of your medication plan, transforming complex polypharmacy into safe, clear, and effective health management.

Comprehensive Review (CMR)

A full 1-on-1 evaluation covering every prescription, OTC medication, vitamin, and herbal remedy you take to ensure complete safety and therapeutic alignment.

Interaction & Conflict Screening

Identification of harmful drug-drug, drug-food, or drug-disease interactions, as well as therapeutic duplication across prescriptions from different providers.

Cost-Saving Opportunities

We analyze formulary tiers, identify high-value generic substitutions, and find cost-effective therapeutic alternatives to lower your monthly out-of-pocket expenses.

Side Effect Management

Experiencing fatigue, dizziness, or stomach upset? We evaluate whether medications are causing adverse symptoms and recommend adjustments to your doctor.

Personal Medication Plan (MAP)

You leave with a clear, updated Personal Medication Record (PMR) and step-by-step Medication Action Plan (MAP) outlining what each drug does and when to take it.

Prescriber Communication

We send detailed clinical summaries and recommended adjustments directly to your physicians, closing the communication loop between specialists.

An Expert Advocate on Your Healthcare Team

According to clinical studies, over 50% of adults taking multiple medications experience medication-related complications that could be prevented with routine clinical pharmacist reviews.

At Ricker Pharmacy, our MTM consultations bridge the gap between your prescribing specialists. We ensure your medications work synergistically, eliminate duplicate prescriptions, and provide you with clear answers to your questions in a relaxed, private setting.

Who Benefits Most from MTM Services?

Medication Therapy Management delivers vital health protection for individuals managing complex conditions or taking multiple daily prescriptions.

Patients Taking 5+ Medications

Polypharmacy • Multiple Specialists • Senior Health
  • Multi-Doctor Alignment: When cardiologists, endocrinologists, and primary care doctors prescribe independently, we harmonize the complete drug list.
  • Deprescribing Opportunities: We identify medications that may no longer be necessary, reducing pill burden and unwanted side effects safely.
  • Preventing Hospital Readmissions: Post-discharge MTM consultations ensure hospital medication changes match your home routine accurately.

Chronic Disease Management

Diabetes • Hypertension • Heart Failure • COPD
  • Treatment Target Monitoring: We evaluate whether your medications are effectively lowering blood pressure, HbA1c, or cholesterol toward clinical targets.
  • Device & Inhaler Technique: Hands-on coaching for insulin pens, glucometers, and respiratory inhalers to ensure maximum drug delivery.
  • Lifestyle Integration: Guidance on timing medications with meals, exercise, and sleep to enhance daily energy and therapeutic efficacy.
