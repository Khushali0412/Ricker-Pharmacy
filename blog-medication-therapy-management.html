Medication Therapy Management: Preventing Drug Conflicts and Optimizing Your Health

TJ Patel, RPh Clinical Pharmacist
July 22, 2026 6 min read
One-on-one comprehensive medication reviews ensure all prescriptions, OTC meds, and supplements work in synergy.

Key Takeaways at a Glance

  • Polypharmacy Risks: Taking 5 or more medications increases the probability of dangerous drug-drug interactions exponentially.
  • Comprehensive Medication Review (CMR): A 30-minute private consultation with a clinical pharmacist to evaluate your entire regimen.
  • Direct Doctor Communication: We work directly with your doctors to eliminate duplicate therapies and switch to cost-effective alternatives.

What Is Medication Therapy Management (MTM)?

As patients manage multiple chronic conditions—such as hypertension, high cholesterol, diabetes, arthritis, or asthma—they often receive prescriptions from multiple specialists. Without a single coordinating clinician, duplicate medications, conflicting side effects, and dangerous drug interactions can easily go unnoticed.

Medication Therapy Management (MTM) is a personalized clinical service provided by Ricker Pharmacy pharmacists to ensure that every medication you take is safe, effective, and necessary.

What Happens During an MTM Consultation?

Full Regimen Audit

Reviewing all prescription drugs, over-the-counter pain relievers, herbal remedies, and vitamins for safety.

Interaction & Duplicate Detection

Identifying hidden conflicts, therapeutic duplication, or medications that worsen other underlying conditions.

Cost Optimization

Identifying lower-cost generic alternatives, manufacturer copay cards, and therapeutic substitutions to lower monthly out-of-pocket expenses.

Personal Medication Action Plan (MAP)

Providing a clear, laminated summary list of your active medications and exact dosage times for you and your doctors.

"Our goal during an MTM review is to give patients absolute peace of mind. You leave with a clear plan, fewer unnecessary pills, and confidence in your daily health."

— TJ Patel, RPh, Clinical Pharmacist at Ricker Pharmacy
