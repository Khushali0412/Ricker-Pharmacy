Personalized Medicine Crafted for Your Unique Health Needs

Prescription compounding is the art and science of preparing customized medications specifically tailored to individual patient requirements. At Ricker Pharmacy, we blend modern pharmaceutical technology with personalized care.

Commercial, mass-manufactured drugs operate on a one-size-fits-all model that doesn't work for every patient. Whether you need a specific dosage strength not commercially available, a unique delivery form, an allergen-free preparation, or a hard-to-find formula, our licensed compounding pharmacists formulate exact solutions in close partnership with your physician.

Our Compounding Capabilities

From custom pediatric liquids and bioidentical hormones to veterinary formulations and topical pain management gels, discover how our compounding lab solves complex medication challenges.

Custom Dosage Strengths

We formulate precise medication strengths tailored to your specific weight, age, or metabolic tolerance when commercially manufactured doses are too strong or too weak.

Alternative Delivery Forms

Transform hard-to-swallow tablets into flavored liquid suspensions, rapid-dissolving sublingual troches, topical transdermal creams, or suppositories for hassle-free treatment.

Allergen & Preservative-Free

Eliminate inactive ingredients that trigger sensitivities. We compound pure formulations completely free of gluten, lactose, artificial dyes, sugars, alcohol, and preservatives.

Combination Medications

Combine multiple active pharmaceutical ingredients into a single, convenient dosage form to simplify your daily regimen, reduce pill burden, and enhance compliance.

Discontinued & Hard-to-Find Drugs

When pharmaceutical manufacturers discontinue vital medications or experience commercial supply shortages, our lab can compound the exact active formulation you rely on.

Specialty & Veterinary Compounds

Specialized treatments for dermatology, podiatry, palliative care, and veterinary compounding with pet-friendly flavors like tuna, chicken, and beef.

The Power of Custom Formulations

Every individual body is biologically distinct. Factors such as body composition, gastrointestinal absorption rates, organ metabolism, and drug sensitivities mean that standard commercial medicines cannot satisfy every clinical scenario.

At Ricker Pharmacy, our compounding pharmacists work as an integral part of your healthcare team—collaborating directly with physicians, specialists, dentists, and veterinarians to compound precise formulations that optimize therapeutic outcomes and minimize adverse side effects.

Our Compounding Process

From physician consultation to precision formulation and patient counseling, every step of our compounding workflow is rigorous, transparent, and quality-controlled.

Prescription & Consultation

01

Your prescriber sends your customized prescription directly to Ricker Pharmacy. Our clinical team reviews your medical history, dosage requirements, and potential allergies.

Formulation Design

02

We select highest-grade Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients (APIs) sourced exclusively from FDA-registered facilities and design the optimal base for stability and absorption.

Precision Compounding

03

Medications are compounded in our dedicated lab following strict USP guidelines, utilizing electronic analytical balances, ointment mills, and certified cleanroom hoods.

Quality Check & Delivery

04

Each batch undergoes rigorous quality verification for potency and weight consistency. We provide comprehensive counseling and convenient pickup or delivery.
