Dental

WELLHealth Rx works with dentists to formulate special dental compounds to meet your unique needs.

We work with several local dentists to help them make dental procedures much more comfortable.

Some of the specialty compounding for Dentists include:

  • Gag Reflux Suppression Medication
  • Anti-Nausea Transdermal Medications
  • Root Canal Pastes
  • Dry Mouth, Stomatitis, and Mucositis Medications
  • Dry Socket Mixtures
  • Miracle Mouthwashes for Aphthous Ulcers, Canker Sores, and Oral Lesions
  • Mouth Rinses that contain Anti Funguls, Anti Virals, Antihistamines, and Anesthetics
  • Transdermal Topical Medications for pain
  • Topical Anesthetics
  • TMJ Medications
  • Pleasantly Flavored Medications
  • Sugar Free, Preservative Free, Lactose Free, Dye Free Medications
  • Unavailable or Hard to Find Medications
  • Pre and Post Procedural Anti Anxiety Medications
  • Bleaching/Whitening Agents

Common questions and answers about our dental compounding services.
