Specialty Compounding for Dentists

We formulate a wide range of specialty dental compounds designed to improve patient comfort before, during, and after dental procedures. Each formulation is customized to meet your unique needs.

Topical Anesthetics

Custom topical anesthetic formulations for enhanced numbing and patient comfort during dental procedures.

Miracle Mouthwashes

Specialized mouthwash compounds for aphthous ulcers, canker sores, and oral lesions with targeted relief.

Dry Socket Mixtures

Custom dry socket preparations and root canal pastes to promote healing and manage post-procedural pain.

Dry Mouth & Stomatitis

Specialized medications for dry mouth, stomatitis, and mucositis conditions that affect oral comfort.

TMJ & Pain Medications

Transdermal topical medications for TMJ disorders and post-procedural pain management without systemic effects.

Anti-Anxiety & Gag Reflex

Pre and post-procedural anti-anxiety medications and gag reflex suppression for anxious dental patients.

More Specialty Dental Compounds

Our compounding capabilities extend beyond the basics. We formulate mouth rinses containing anti-fungals, anti-virals, antihistamines, and anesthetics for comprehensive oral care. We also prepare bleaching and whitening agents tailored to your practice's protocols.

We specialize in creating sugar-free, preservative-free, lactose-free, and dye-free medications for patients with sensitivities, as well as unavailable or hard-to-find medications that are no longer commercially manufactured.

Common Dental Dosage Forms

We compound dental medications in a wide variety of delivery forms, ensuring each formulation is optimized for maximum effectiveness, patient comfort, and ease of use.

Ointments, Pastes & Gels

01

Dental ointments, pastes, and oral/topical gels for targeted application directly to affected areas for fast-acting localized relief.

Mouthwashes & Rinses

02

Custom mouthwashes and rinses with anti-fungal, anti-viral, antihistamine, and anesthetic agents for comprehensive oral treatment.

Troches & Sublinguals

03

Troches, sublingual tablets, lollipops, and mucosal bandages (Polyoxyl) designed to dissolve slowly for sustained medication delivery in the oral cavity.

Sprays, Balms & More

04

Oral and topical sprays, lip balms, chapstick, powder puffers, capsules, and even popsicles—creative delivery forms for maximum patient comfort.
