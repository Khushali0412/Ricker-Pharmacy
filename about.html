ABOUT US

Who We Are

Are you tired of going store to store just to complete your prescription list? Still having difficulty in finding a pharmacy that you can trust? Here at RICKER PHARMACY we acknowledge that every person is unique and requires special attention depending on their situation. Our goal is to provide the utmost professional and personal quality of service as quickly and efficiently as possible.

RICKER PHARMACY is a locally owned and operated pharmacy and we've been serving Jacksonville and the surrounding areas since 2010. We strive to provide a family-like environment for all our patients. We want you to feel as though you are a part of our family, so we have set a high standard of care and compassion for our patients and treat them as we would our own family.

Although we are a small independent pharmacy, we want to offer our customers the same range of services provided by the big chains, but with personal attention and the highest standard of customer service sometimes lacking in the retail chains today. We have a firm standard of no discrimination based on religion, gender, race, age, social status, or handicap. Rest assured that you will be treated well and in a way that is above and beyond our competitors.
