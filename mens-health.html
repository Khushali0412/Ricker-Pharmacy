About Men's Health

Just as women go through menopause, which causes a decrease in hormones, SO DO MEN.

Male Menopause, also called ANDROPAUSE, is not nearly as dramatic as the female version. Some men, though, may actually go through a rather sudden change in testosterone levels that might correlate with the hormonal changes that women experience during menopause. However, most men experience a slower, more subtle hormone decline.

When hormones are replaced or restored to physiologic levels considered normal for younger males, older men may experience a dramatic reversal of many of the changes caused by aging. Natural Bio-Identical Hormone Replacement Therapy in men involves the use of the hormone Testosterone. Please note that Natural Testosterone should not be confused with synthetic derivatives. Testosterone deficiency in males has been an unrecognized syndrome for many years, possibly because the blood levels of testosterone in men decrease over time.

Andropause Replacement Therapy may also include the addition of Pregnenolone, DHEA (Dehydroepiandrosterone), Aromatase Inhibitors, Chrysin, Dihydrotestosterone, Zinc, Selenium, and other supplements. There are many symptoms of testosterone deficiency that are sometimes referred to as the “grumpy man syndrome”.

Testosterone deficiency may consist of a variety of signs and symptoms including

Reduced libido or the reduced desire for sex

A noticeable drop in sexual interest, passion, and intimate desire, often accompanied by reduced overall vitality.

Fatigue or a decrease in physical stamina

Persistent exhaustion and loss of endurance that makes workouts, daily work, and routine tasks feel significantly harder.

Difficulty in maintaining erections

Struggles with achieving or maintaining firm erections, directly linked to declining circulating testosterone levels.

A decrease in mental sharpness, or memory impairment

Brain fog, difficulty concentrating on complex tasks, and occasional lapses in short-term memory or cognitive focus.

Weight gain

Increased body fat, particularly around the abdomen and midsection, despite maintaining a consistent diet and routine.

Feelings of depression and anxiety

Unexpected shifts in emotional well-being, unexplained anxiety, persistent low moods, or loss of motivation and drive.

Heart disease

Unbalanced hormone levels can negatively impact vascular elasticity, lipid profiles, and long-term cardiovascular health.

Reduced muscle mass

Loss of lean muscle tone and diminished physical strength even when regularly exercising or lifting weights.

Irritability

Quick temper, heightened agitation, and mood instability commonly associated with the "grumpy man syndrome."

Weakness

Noticeable decrease in physical power, grip strength, and overall functional capacity throughout the day.

Disturbed Sleep

Frequent nighttime awakenings, difficulty falling asleep, insomnia, or waking up unrefreshed despite hours in bed.

Osteoporosis

Gradual loss of bone mineral density, weakening skeletal structure and increasing long-term susceptibility to fractures.

Atherosclerosis

Narrowing or hardening of arterial walls, which can be accelerated by sub-optimal androgen and metabolic balance.

Low sperm count

Decreased semen volume and lower sperm production affecting fertility, vitality, and reproductive health.

Slow wound healing

Cuts, scrapes, and muscular injuries taking longer than usual to repair due to reduced cellular regeneration.

Common questions and answers about men's health and andropause hormone therapy.

Andropause, also called Male Menopause, is a gradual decline in testosterone levels that occurs as men age. Unlike female menopause, which involves a relatively sudden hormonal shift, most men experience a slower, more subtle hormone decline over time. Some men, however, may experience a more dramatic change similar to what women experience.

Natural Bio-Identical Hormone Replacement Therapy uses testosterone that is chemically identical to what your body naturally produces. This should not be confused with synthetic derivatives. Bio-identical testosterone is custom-compounded to match your individual hormone levels and needs.
