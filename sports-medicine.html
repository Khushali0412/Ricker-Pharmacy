Custom Sports Medicine

Through custom compounding, WELLHealth Rx can assist physicians and sports trainers in alleviating inflammation, spasms, cramps and muscle pain related to sports injuries. With special compounded medicines, we make it easy to treat:

Blisters- with flexible collodion preparations
Fever Blisters- lip balms with or without sunblock
Dehydration- with rehydrating sports drink formulas
Athlete's Foot-with topical antifungals
Muscle Aches- with topical gels
Muscle Cramps- with speed gels
Wounds, with polyol bandages, tissue regenerating gels, and protective ointments
Carpal Tunnel- with transdermal gels
Nail Fungus- with topical antifungal solutions
Hemorrhoids- with rectal suppositories and ointments
Sprains and Trigger Point- with transdermal gels

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions and answers about our sports medicine compounding services.

We compound medications to treat a wide range of sports-related conditions including muscle aches and cramps, sprains and trigger points, blisters and fever blisters, athlete's foot, nail fungus, carpal tunnel, wounds, dehydration, and hemorrhoids—all with customized formulations.

Transdermal gels are applied directly to the skin over the affected area, delivering medication locally where it's needed most. This targeted approach provides faster relief at the injury site while significantly reducing systemic side effects compared to oral medications.
