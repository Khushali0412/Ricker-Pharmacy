Custom Sports Medicine Compounding

Through custom compounding, Ricker Pharmacy can assist physicians and sports trainers in alleviating inflammation, spasms, cramps and muscle pain related to sports injuries.

With special compounded medicines, we make it easy to treat a wide range of sports-related conditions—from blisters and muscle aches to carpal tunnel and nail fungus. Our formulations are designed to get athletes back in the game faster with targeted, effective relief.

Conditions We Treat

Our compounding pharmacists create specialized formulations to address the most common sports-related injuries and conditions, helping athletes recover faster and perform better.

Muscle Aches & Cramps

Custom topical gels and speed gels for rapid relief of muscle aches, cramps, spasms, and inflammation from overuse or injury.

Blisters & Fever Blisters

Flexible collodion preparations for blisters and custom lip balms with or without sunblock for fever blister prevention and healing.

Sprains & Trigger Points

Transdermal gels delivering anti-inflammatory and analgesic agents directly to sprain and trigger point areas for targeted relief.

Athlete's Foot & Nail Fungus

Topical antifungal preparations and antifungal solutions customized to effectively treat athlete's foot and stubborn nail fungus.

Wounds & Tissue Repair

Polyol bandages, tissue regenerating gels, and protective ointments to promote rapid wound healing and recovery.

Carpal Tunnel & More

Transdermal gels for carpal tunnel relief, rehydrating sports drink formulas for dehydration, and rectal suppositories for hemorrhoids.

Why Choose Ricker for Sports Medicine?

Targeted Pain & Injury Treatment

Topical Delivery • Reduced Side Effects • Fast Acting
  • Localized Treatment: Transdermal gels and topical formulations deliver medication directly to the injury site, reducing systemic exposure and minimizing side effects.
  • Rapid Absorption: Speed gels and fast-absorbing transdermal preparations provide quick relief for muscle cramps, spasms, and acute inflammation.
  • Multi-Condition Coverage: From blisters and sprains to carpal tunnel and nail fungus, we compound for the full spectrum of sports-related conditions.

Recovery & Performance Support

  • Advanced Wound Care: Tissue regenerating gels, polyol bandages, and protective ointments promote rapid healing for cuts, abrasions, and sports wounds.
