All Your Prescriptions, One Single Day Each Month

Tired of making multiple trips to the pharmacy every few weeks? Medication Synchronization (Med Sync) coordinates all your chronic and maintenance prescriptions so they are refilled together on the exact same date each month.

At Ricker Pharmacy, our Med Sync program is completely free and customized to your schedule. We review your full profile, contact your prescribers for renewals in advance, and prepare your entire monthly supply in advance—giving you hassle-free, organized medication management.

Key Advantages of Med Sync

Discover how synchronizing your monthly prescriptions eliminates hassle, enhances safety, and keeps your health on track.

One Convenient Monthly Trip

Say goodbye to driving to the pharmacy multiple times a month. Pick up your complete prescription supply in a single visit, saving you valuable time and fuel.

Proactive Physician Coordination

We monitor your remaining refills and reach out directly to your doctors ahead of time for renewals, so you never experience unexpected treatment gaps.

Monthly Pharmacist Review

A few days before your sync date, our pharmacist calls to check for hospital visits, doctor changes, or new symptoms, ensuring your regimen is 100% accurate.

Bridging & Alignment Support

We work with your insurance to coordinate short-fill bridging doses when necessary, aligning disparate renewal dates smoothly without cost surprises.

Optional Blister Packaging

Take convenience further by combining Med Sync with our custom adherence packaging—receiving your synchronized medications pre-sorted by date and time.

Pickup or Local Delivery

Enjoy quick, ready-and-waiting in-store pickup on your chosen sync date, or have your synchronized medications delivered directly to your Jacksonville home.

Peace of Mind for Patients & Caregivers

Managing several maintenance medications with different expiration and refill cycles is a frequent cause of accidental non-adherence. Running out of a vital heart or diabetes drug over the weekend can be dangerous and stressful.

With Ricker Pharmacy’s Med Sync service, our clinical staff takes over the entire calendar. We synchronize your dates, resolve insurance hurdles, and package everything together so you never have to worry about missing a dose or making an emergency pharmacy run.

How to Get Started in 4 Easy Steps

Enrolling in Med Sync takes just a few minutes. Here is how our pharmacists streamline your entire medication calendar.

Free Enrollment

01

Speak with our pharmacy team in-store or over the phone. We review your current list of maintenance prescriptions and over-the-counter supplements.

Pick Your Sync Date

02

Select the day of the month that works best for you (e.g., the 1st or 15th). We align your refills with bridging doses to match your chosen target date.
