Customized Hormone Replacement Therapy (BHRT): Finding Balance and Vitality

Shawn Nelson, PharmD Compounding Specialist
October 02, 2026 6 min read
Custom compounded bioidentical hormones match the exact molecular structure of human hormones.

Key Takeaways at a Glance

  • Molecular Exactness: Bioidentical hormones (estrogen, progesterone, testosterone, DHEA) are chemically identical to hormones produced naturally by the human body.
  • Custom Dosage Formats: Formulated as topical creams, sublingual troches, vaginal creams, or slow-release capsules tailored to exact lab values.
  • Triad Collaboration: Pharmacist, patient, and prescribing physician collaborate to continuously fine-tune dosages as symptoms improve.

The Impact of Hormonal Imbalance

As we age, natural hormone production gradually decreases. For women experiencing perimenopause or menopause, this decline leads to debilitating symptoms including hot flashes, night sweats, brain fog, sleep disturbances, mood swings, and low libido. For men (andropause), low testosterone causes fatigue, reduced muscle mass, weight gain, and diminished drive.

Standard commercial hormone medications offer limited "one-size-fits-all" doses and often use synthetic progestins or equine-derived estrogens that some patients tolerate poorly.

What Makes BHRT Different?

Bioidentical Hormone Replacement Therapy (BHRT) utilizes plant-derived substances (commonly from wild yams and soy) that are chemically synthesized to duplicate the precise molecular structure of human hormones:

Bi-Est & Tri-Est (Estrogens)

Tailored blends of Estradiol (E2) and Estriol (E3) to relieve vasomotor symptoms, protect bone density, and support cardiovascular health.

Bioidentical Progesterone

Calms the nervous system, supports deep restorative sleep, protects uterine lining, and balances estrogen dominance.

Testosterone (Men & Women)

Essential for mental clarity, lean muscle maintenance, metabolic rate, physical stamina, and libido.

DHEA & Thyroid Support

Adrenal and metabolic optimization supporting cellular energy, immune resilience, and healthy aging.

Personalized Delivery Formats

Compounding allows us to bypass the liver (first-pass metabolism) to deliver smooth, consistent hormone absorption:

  • Transdermal Creams & Gels: Absorbed directly through the skin via metered-dose Topi-CLICK applicators.
  • Sublingual Troches / Lozenges: Dissolve slowly under the tongue or cheek for rapid systemic uptake.
  • Slow-Release Oral Capsules: Designed to maintain steady hormone levels throughout the day or night.

"Hormone optimization is not about massive doses—it is about restoring physiological balance. When patients receive the exact ratio their body needs, the improvement in quality of life is transformative."

— Shawn Nelson, PharmD, Compounding Specialist at Ricker Pharmacy
