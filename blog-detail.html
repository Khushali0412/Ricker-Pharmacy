Understanding Autologous Serum Eye Drops (ASED): The Natural Cure for Severe Dry Eyes

TJ Patel, RPh Clinical Compounding Pharmacist
October 14, 2026 5 min read
Sterile lab preparation of customized 20%–50% ASED vials at Ricker Pharmacy.

Key Takeaways at a Glance

  • 100% Biocompatible: Created from your own purified blood serum, eliminating allergic risks and stinging.
  • Biological Healing: Rich in Epidermal Growth Factor (EGF), Vitamin A, Fibronectin, and Albumin that artificial tears cannot mimic.
  • Sterile Compounding: Prepared in Ricker Pharmacy's USP <797> cleanroom to ensure absolute sterility and optimal concentration.

The Limits of Conventional Artificial Tears

Millions of Americans suffer from chronic Dry Eye Disease (DED), experiencing persistent gritty sensations, burning, blurred vision, and light sensitivity. For many patients, over-the-counter lubricating eye drops only offer temporary, superficial moisture.

Commercial artificial tears lack essential biochemical components found in natural human tears. Furthermore, frequent use of preservative-laden eye drops can cause corneal toxicity, leading to epithelial damage and worsened ocular inflammation over time.

What Are Autologous Serum Eye Drops (ASED)?

Autologous Serum Eye Drops (ASED) are custom-formulated ophthalmic drops made directly from a patient’s own blood serum. Because blood serum and natural tears share nearly identical biochemical properties, pH, and osmolarity, ASED represents the closest biological substitute to healthy tears.

Standard Eye Drops

  • Synthetic lubricants (carboxymethylcellulose)
  • No cellular growth factors or healing proteins
  • Contains chemical preservatives (in multi-dose bottles)
  • Provides temporary surface hydration only

Autologous Serum Drops

  • 100% natural, patient-derived serum
  • Loaded with EGF, TGF-β, Fibronectin & Albumin
  • Completely preservative-free & non-toxic
  • Actively repairs and regenerates corneal nerves

Biological Growth Factors: How Serum Heals the Eye

Human serum contains a potent mixture of biologically active elements essential for ocular surface maintenance and epithelial cell regeneration:

Epidermal Growth Factor (EGF)

Accelerates the healing rate of damaged corneal and conjunctival epithelial cells.

Immunoglobulins (IgA, IgG) & Lysozymes

Provides powerful natural antimicrobial defense against surface ocular pathogens.

Fibronectin & Substance P

Promotes cellular adhesion, anchoring new epithelial cells firmly to the cornea.

Albumin & Vitamin A

Inhibits damaging inflammatory enzymes and stabilizes the pre-corneal tear film.

How Ricker Pharmacy Prepares Your Serum Drops

At Ricker Pharmacy, the preparation of autologous serum drops is carried out with meticulous adherence to sterile compounding standards:

  1. Convenient Blood Draw: A simple blood sample is drawn from the patient via our streamlined on-site or partnered lab protocol.
  2. Clotting & Centrifugation: The blood is allowed to clot naturally before being spun in a medical-grade centrifuge to cleanly isolate the clear serum from red blood cells.
  3. Sterile Dilution: In our certified ISO Class 5 laminar airflow hood, our pharmacists dilute the serum (commonly 20%, 30%, 40%, or 50% concentration) with sterile, preservative-free saline.
  4. Unit Packaging & Freezing: The solution is divided into UV-shielded amber dropper vials and frozen immediately to preserve growth factor stability for up to 6 months.

"When conventional therapies fail, autologous serum drops offer patients real, physiological recovery. Because the medication is derived entirely from their own body, tolerance is virtually 100%."

— TJ Patel, RPh, Senior Pharmacist at Ricker Pharmacy

Who Is an Ideal Candidate for ASED?

Ophthalmologists and corneal specialists frequently prescribe Autologous Serum Eye Drops for patients with:

  • Severe Dry Eye Syndrome (Aqueous-Deficient or Evaporative) resistant to artificial tears and punctal plugs.
  • Sjögren’s Syndrome & Autoimmune Ocular Disease causing chronic severe tear deficiency.
  • Persistent Epithelial Defects (PED) and recurrent corneal erosions.
  • Neurotrophic Keratopathy with impaired corneal sensation.
  • Post-LASIK or Post-Corneal Transplant delayed healing.

Storage & Usage Instructions for Patients

Because ASED contains no artificial chemical preservatives, proper temperature control is essential:

  • Current In-Use Bottle: Store in the refrigerator (2°C to 8°C) and use within 7 to 10 days.
  • Reserve Bottles: Store unopened dropper bottles in your home freezer (-20°C) for up to 3 to 6 months.
  • Instillation Tip: Instill 1 drop into the affected eye(s) 4 to 8 times daily, or as precisely directed by your eye care physician.
