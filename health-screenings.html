Our Preventive Health Screenings

From cardiovascular metrics and diabetes monitoring to infection diagnostics, our pharmacy offers comprehensive screening solutions for your family.

Blood Pressure Screening

Fast, automated blood pressure checks to screen for hypertension, track treatment progress, and assess cardiovascular health with immediate pharmacist evaluation.

Glucose & HbA1c Testing

Fingerstick blood glucose checks and 3-month average HbA1c testing for early diabetes detection, prediabetes monitoring, and ongoing glycemic management.

Cholesterol & Lipid Panel

Comprehensive lipid screening measuring Total Cholesterol, HDL (good), LDL (bad), and Triglycerides from a single fingerstick, with results ready in under 10 minutes.

Body Composition & BMI

Assessment of Body Mass Index (BMI), body fat percentage, and baseline metabolic metrics to support personalized weight management and fitness goals.

Bone Density Screening

Non-invasive ultrasound heel bone mineral density checks to assess osteoporosis risk and evaluate bone strength in postmenopausal women and older adults.

Point-of-Care Infection Tests

Rapid diagnostic testing for Strep Throat, Influenza A/B, and COVID-19 with fast results, helping you get the right treatment without unnecessary delays.

Early Detection is the Best Medicine

Many of the most serious chronic health conditions—including high blood pressure, elevated cholesterol, and type 2 diabetes—often develop quietly with zero noticeable symptoms in their early stages.

Regular health screenings at Ricker Pharmacy empower you to understand your baseline health, identify potential risks before they cause complications, and work proactively with our clinical team and your physician on lifestyle and medication optimizations.

How Our Screenings Work

Getting screened at Ricker Pharmacy is convenient, fast, and confidential. Here is what your visit looks like.

Walk In or Call

01

Walk in anytime during pharmacy hours or schedule ahead. Let our team know which screening you would like to complete today.

Quick Sample Collection

02

Our trained pharmacist performs a gentle fingerstick or non-invasive check in our private, comfortable clinical consultation room.

Instant Lab Analysis

03

Our certified, CLIA-waived diagnostic analyzers process your sample on-site, delivering lab-accurate results in just 5 to 10 minutes.

Consultation & Report

04

Our pharmacist reviews what your numbers mean, offers lifestyle advice, and gives you a comprehensive printed report for your physician.
